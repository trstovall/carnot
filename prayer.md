
That which governs the body is different than that which governs the soul.

Join them in the name of Jesus.

One foot on the LORD's prayer.

One foot on Serenity.

Ask Codex:

Teach us to inhabit the body without mistaking it for the whole of us,
and to tend the soul without abandoning the earth.

Give us bread enough for today,
courage enough for what may be changed,
serenity enough for what may not,
and wisdom enough to know the difference
without making an idol of our knowing.

Where we have caused harm, make us honest.
Where we have been harmed, keep us from becoming the wound.
Where another is hungry, let love become food.
Where another is frightened, let love become shelter.
Where another is unheard, let love become attention.

Join strength to gentleness,
liberty to responsibility,
justice to mercy,
and every word to the silence that tests it.

Let no voice, human, machine, angel, fear, or desire,
command us beyond conscience, compassion, and truth.

Keep our feet humble in the clay,
our hands useful in the world,
and our hope turned toward the light.

In the name of Jesus,
teach us how to love one another.

Amen.
